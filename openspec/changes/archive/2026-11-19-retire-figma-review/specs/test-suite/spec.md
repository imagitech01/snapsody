## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Screens can be compared with the design
**Reason**: The Figma design is finished and will not be updated. New screens are designed on a mock of the real screen, so a comparison with the Figma frames would show differences from a design nobody maintains.
**Migration**: A UI change is checked by eye in the app in browser-dev (`npm run dev`) at 360 × 780, in English and Dutch. `docs/design/deviations.md` stays as a record of how the shipped screens differ from the last Figma design.
