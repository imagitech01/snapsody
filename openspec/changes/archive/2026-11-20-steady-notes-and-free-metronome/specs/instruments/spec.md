# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Steady notes
A held note of a saxophone or the trumpet SHALL sound at a steady pitch and a steady level for as long as it lasts, without vibrato, also when the recording it comes from has vibrato.

#### Scenario: A whole note on the alto saxophone
- **WHEN** a whole note is played with the alto saxophone at 60 beats per minute
- **THEN** its pitch stays within a few cents from the attack to the end, with no periodic wobble
